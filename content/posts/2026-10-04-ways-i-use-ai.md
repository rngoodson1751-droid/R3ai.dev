People ask me what I actually use AI for. The honest answer is "almost everything, a little." That is not helpful to someone who has never tried it.

So I asked Claude to go back through our work together and list every kind of thing we have done. It came back with 27. Each one is now its own short post, built around a real example.

These are about how I use AI. For the tools and games themselves, see the project write-ups on the [At work](/work/) and [At home](/home/) pages.

## How to read these

Pick the one closest to your own life. Each post follows the same pattern: what the situation was, what I asked, what came back and where it fell short. I kept that last part in every post on purpose.

If you only read one, read [When the AI is wrong, and when it says no](/blog/when-ai-is-wrong-and-when-it-says-no/).
