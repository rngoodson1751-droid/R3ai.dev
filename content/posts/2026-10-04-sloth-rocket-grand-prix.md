The brief was a go-kart racer in a single HTML file, with five themes that have nothing to do with each other: Formula 1, rockets, pirates, libraries and sloths. I gave that to Claude on a Sunday morning and had a playable game before lunch.

[Play Sloth Rocket Grand Prix](/games/sloth-rocket-grand-prix/)

## What's in it

- **Five courses.** Pole Position Park, Launch Pad Loop, Cutthroat Cove, The Grand Stacks and Slowpoke Canopy.
- **Five karts.** A formula car, an astronaut on a rocket, a pirate galleon, a librarian's book cart and a sloth on a log. Each one handles differently.
- **Up to four players** on one screen, with computer rivals filling the rest of the grid.
- **Three levels** (Rookie, Pro and Legend) and a five-race Grand Prix with points.
- **Items.** Turbo, cannonball, book trap, heat shield and the sloth spell, which slows down everyone ahead of you.

## How to play

Open the game in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

- **Xbox controller:** press A to join. RT or A accelerates, LT or B brakes, RB drifts, and LB or X uses an item.
- **Keyboard:** press W or the up arrow to join. Steer with WASD or the arrows, drift with Space or Shift, and use items with E or Enter.
- **Wheel and pedals:** press any button on the wheel to join. A short calibration walks through steering, gas and brake.

Hold drift through a corner and let go for a boost.

## Where it falls short

- The graphics are simple blocks. There are no artist-made models.
- A wheel gets no force feedback, because browsers don't offer it.
- I haven't tried it on an Xbox console yet. It should run in the console's Edge browser, but that's a guess until I test it.
