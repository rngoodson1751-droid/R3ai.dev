River loves rockets, and Starship most of all. So I built him a space program.

[Play River's Rocket Works](/games/rivers-rocket-works/)

## How it works

You design a rocket in the workshop, then fly it. There are twelve missions, and they get bigger as you go.

1. First Flight
2. Edge of Space
3. Come Home Booster
4. Orbit!
5. Station Delivery
6. Moon Landing
7. Mars Rover
8. Rings of Saturn
9. Red Spot Rally
10. The Wormhole Run
11. The Rainbow Race
12. Asteroid Blaster

There's also a free flight mode for when you only want to fly around.

## Built for a kid and a grown-up together

The game talks. A voice reads out every mission, so a kid who is still learning to read can play it. There's a co-pilot assist that quietly helps with the hard parts, so nobody ends the night frustrated. It works with an Xbox controller, a keyboard or a touch screen, and it remembers your stars in the browser.

Finish the missions and there's a certificate at the end.

## The Rainbow Race

This is the silliest mission. A glowing rainbow track circles the whole solar system and you pick your racer.

- River, on a shooting star.
- Mom, on her unicorn.
- Dad, in a Viking ship pulled by goats.
- Justice Ruth Bader Ginsburg, our white Labrador, in her lace collar.

Yes, that's her full name. We call her Justice.

## What I learned

A game like this can carry real ideas. Getting to orbit isn't only about going up. You have to go sideways, very fast, and the Orbit mission makes you do it. A kid who has flown that once has learned something a diagram wouldn't teach.

It's one web page built on Three.js. If it runs slowly, try it on a computer before a phone.
