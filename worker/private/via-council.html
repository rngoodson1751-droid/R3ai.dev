TODAY'S PLAN

    The short version

    One project. Three winners.
    Affordable transportation for EVERYONE.

    🏛️
    FOR THE CITYBetter service at a known cost
    • Mostly federal dollars. Federal transit funds pay most of the cost. The City share includes the $148,300 startup, from unspent FY26 Transit capital funds.
    • New procurement innovation. Lake Charles is among the first cities in Louisiana to use the new state law (Act 665 of 2026) to buy services through a purchasing cooperative. Our department brought it to the City, and Legal and Finance vetted it.
    • A 2-year contract, not a permanent commitment. Council approves it and decides what comes next.
    🚌
    FOR THE TRANSIT DEPARTMENTMore reach from the same team
    • Vans, drivers and software provided by Via as a service.
    • Paratransit modernized: real-time booking and dispatch instead of voicemail and phone tag.
    • Trip-by-trip data to plan service and back up our federal reporting.
    🏘️
    FOR YOUR CONSTITUENTSA ride from near the front door
    • No long walk to a fixed line. Pickup at a nearby corner, drop-off close to where they are going.
    • Nights and weekends. Rides until 9 pm on weekdays and all weekend. Today's buses stop at 5:45 pm and don't run on weekends.
    • Book by app, online, or a phone call, so riders without smartphones are not left out.
    • Jobs, groceries, doctor visits: the trips that are hardest today for seniors, riders with disabilities, and families without a car.
